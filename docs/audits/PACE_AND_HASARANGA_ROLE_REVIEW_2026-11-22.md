# Pace redundancy and Hasaranga role review — 22 November 2026

## Retained pace role map

The dashboard now describes the retained attack as a role portfolio rather than an empty pace-bowling slot:

- **Mohammed Shami and Nandre Burger:** preferred new-ball / powerplay pair in the current analyst role map (right-arm and left-arm options).
- **Jofra Archer:** premium strike option with new-ball quality and credible death-over flexibility.
- **Sandeep Sharma:** control, variations and experienced phase cover.
- **Brijesh Sharma:** Indian pace depth with a retained death-over option.
- **Sushant Mishra:** developmental left-arm reserve / rotation depth.

The recruitment implication is **selective pace insurance**, not an urgent headcount vacancy. A further quick is an advantage only if he improves workload / injury resilience or provides a distinct phase skill. Boult is a historical new-ball reference, not evidence that RR currently has no new-ball options.

## Hasaranga considered, not prioritised

Wanindu Hasaranga is a plausible skill-set consideration because he combines attacking leg-spin with potential lower-order hitting / batting utility; this is not proof of dependable IPL No.6 finishing output. RR already have wrist-spin through Ravi Bishnoi and Yash Raj Punja, and left-arm orthodox spin through Ravindra Jadeja and Manimaran Siddharth. He therefore does not fill a clean spin vacancy.

The official 18 November 2026 squad snapshot has five overseas players retained and three overseas squad slots remaining. Hasaranga would consume one of those slots, which has an opportunity cost against overseas batting / pace options.

The availability caution is based on public injury interruptions, not a diagnosis of present fitness: ICC reported a left-hamstring injury in August 2024 and a recurring left-hamstring issue in November 2024; he missed IPL 2024 with a heel injury; Daily FT reported a further hamstring concern during the 2026 T20 World Cup and a prior hamstring operation in 2023. Any future consideration requires current independent medical clearance.

**Decision:** do not prioritise in the base plan. Revisit only if the final registered pool, current medical assessment and resulting XI show a clear upgrade after accounting for the overseas-slot cost.
